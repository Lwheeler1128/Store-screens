5-store SOS speed contest · Col O, car-weighted

Drive-Thru Speed Contest

Fastest drive-thru time wins. Times are Col O (Lane Total with Pull Forward) from each store's weekly SOS by Shift report. Lower is better.

How to read this

Col O
Total drive-thru time per car, from pulling into the lane to leaving the window, pull-forwards included. Lower is better.
Days
Days you were credited as the manager on shift, meaning you covered at least half of a daypart. You need 4 days every week to place.
Cars
Drive-thru cars served on your credited dayparts. Busy shifts count more than slow ones, so a strong dinner rush moves your number more than a slow open.
Behind 1st / Behind next
How much time per car you need to cut to catch the leader, or to pass the person one spot ahead.

Where your time goes

Lane Queue is where the race is won. Menu Time barely moves between managers, but Lane Queue swings the most from first place to last. Keeping the line moving between the menu board and the window explains most of the gap. Green marks the 3 fastest in the group for each step, red the 3 slowest.

Contest rules

Raw time winsEveryone is ranked on fastest Col O across all 5 stores, not against their store's goal.
4-day minimumA manager needs at least 4 days credited in every week to hold a place. Short weeks show as not qualified.
Car-weighted averageThe 4-week time is weighted by cars served, so a busy week counts more than a light one.
RosterDistrict manager, managers in training and new hires not yet running shifts are left off. Managers who cover other stores are combined into one entry.
